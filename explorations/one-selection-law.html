One Selection Law Connects World Symmetry to LLM Behavior

A critical review of Sean Butner's Grounded Misprision (Nov 2026) and a unified four-stage research program linking physical symmetries, cultural corpus accumulation, Transformer representation geometry, and verifier-grounded reasoning.

Verdict: Grounded Misprision supplies the missing middle link in the theory of language models. By deriving misreading from rate-distortion limits (T1) and separating grounded expansion from closed-loop collapse via the data-processing inequality (T2–T4), it explains how a finite, noisy human corpus preserves world structure across generations. To unify the entire path from statistical symmetry to LLM capabilities, however, five formal seams must be repaired—most importantly pragmatic symmetry breaking, adaptive verifier overfitting, the jump from quadratic PMI embeddings to contextual Transformer belief states, and the architectural role of chain-of-thought as external memory.

Stage 1 · World Symmetry

Group-invariant physical and social regularities shape experience, transferred into sign co-occurrence via reference modulo pragmatic bias.

G ↷ Ω ⇒ M(g·i, g·j) ≈ M(i, j)

Stage 2 · Grounded Corpus

Narrow channels force misreading; independent sensory tests and archival re-reading keep grounded mutual information growing.

E[ΔL] = Δ_pool + Δ_select − Δ_drift

Stage 3 · LLM Geometry

Next-token prediction recovers group irreps, hierarchical polytopes, and residual-stream belief simplices as a static snapshot of the corpus.

D_t → Θ_t (irreps + belief states)

Stage 4 · LLM Behavior

Chain-of-thought unlocks deduction at fixed grounded info; only external verifiers supply the independent selection channel needed to expand.

λ > λ* ⇒ expand · λ < λ* ⇒ collapse

Grounded Misprision Solves the Corpus Bottleneck, With Five Formal Seams

The draft's strongest move is replacing axiomatic definitions of creative and destructive misreading with a single information-theoretic mechanism. Treating speech as a ~39 bits/s rate-limited channel (Coupé et al. 2019) makes misprision mathematically unavoidable (T1), while the data-processing inequality immediately forces closed-loop talk to stagnate or decay (T2).

Equally sharp is its resolution of the symbol-grounding debate: meaning from form is possible as a state and impossible as a process. Because reference imprints world symmetry onto co-occurrence, a corpus learner can inherit world geometry as a snapshot, yet cannot refresh it without an independent evidence channel.

Where the draft strains is at the interfaces—especially where cultural-evolution primitives meet modern Transformer theory and post-training dynamics. Five specific seams need repair before Lean formalization or empirical testing.

  1. Adaptive verifier overfitting breaks Δ_select ≥ 0 (T3).

    Held-out validation is non-negative in expectation only for a fixed hypothesis tested once on fresh data. In iterated selection—whether cultural canonization or RL post-training—reusing a fixed verifier turns selection into Goodhart's law. T3 needs a finite-sample generalization or adaptive-data-analysis penalty, tying directly into the verification-quality phase transition proved by Feng et al. (2025).

  2. Pragmatic reporting bias breaks exact equivariance (T8).

    World G-invariance plus reference does not produce exact G-invariant co-occurrence. Speakers compress utterances under Information Bottleneck efficiency (Zaslavsky et al. 2018) and Gricean salience, systematically over-reporting rare anomalies. T8 should be weakened to approximate equivariance with an explicit pragmatic distortion bound—consistent with Storan, Tolias & Miolane (2026) finding that T/I symmetry captures R² = 0.822 (not 1.0) of Bach chorale co-occurrence.

  3. Quadratic PMI embeddings do not automatically explain Transformer world models (T8 → LLM).

    Both Karkada et al. (2026) and Storan et al. (2026) derive harmonic irrep geometry for static, quadratic co-occurrence factorizations. Deep autoregressive Transformers go far beyond pairwise PMI: they represent hierarchical categories as polytopes (Park et al. 2024), multi-dimensional circular manifolds (Engels et al. 2024), and Bayesian belief-state simplices over latent dynamics (Shai et al. 2024).

  4. Fixed-depth Transformers are not universal interpreters without external scratchpads (A9 / T7).

    The draft rightly notes that Turing universality belongs to "hearer + paper," not syntax alone. For LLMs, that distinction is mathematically sharp: fixed-depth Transformers without intermediate tokens cannot simulate arbitrary sequential computation, whereas adding Chain-of-Thought (CoT) tokens acts as an external tape that unlocks polynomial-time and P-complete computation (Merrill & Sabharwal 2024). A9 maps cleanly onto CoT.

  5. The empirical anchor for RLHF sophistry is contested (C29–C30).

    The draft cites Wen et al. (2025) (+24.1% QA and +18.3% coding evaluator false-positive rates) as proof of RLHF-driven sophistry. However, an April 2026 ICLR Blogposts replication audit found implementation bugs in that RLHF pipeline and failed to reproduce the effect on a corrected run. Conversely, Guo, Wu & Yiu (2026)—cited from abstract only in the draft—provides stronger full-paper evidence for T3: across 10 self-training generations of LLaMA-2-7B and Mistral-7B in three languages, task-grounded filtering sustained compositionality (Hedges' g > 1.6) while LLM regularization gradients matched human iterated-learning data at R² = 0.94.

Four Stages Unify Physical Symmetry, Corpus Accumulation, and LLM Capabilities

Rather than treating embedding geometry, cultural language evolution, mechanistic interpretability, and post-training alignment as separate subfields, the entire path can be formulated as one coupled dynamical system over four linked transformations: World (Ω, G) → Corpus Archive (D_t) → Pretrained Weights & Representations (Θ_t) → Inference & Post-Trained Policies (π_t).

This single pair of relations reconciles the central paradox of modern LLMs. Pretraining on human language extracts extraordinary latent structure—world maps, calendars, board states, and multi-skill composition—because D_t already embodies millennia of grounded human selection. Yet without an independent verification channel E_ver (execution, formal proof checking, or physical experiment), post-training cannot increase grounded mutual information I(Ω; ·); it can only expand explicit deductive content via Chain-of-Thought steps T(n), or else drift into low-pass spectral collapse.

Three Formal Bridges Complete the Path From Symmetry to Behavior

To move from a collection of suggestive analogies to a single unified theory, the research program must explicitly construct three mathematical bridges connecting adjacent stages.

Bridge A · Stage 1 → Stage 3

Group Actions, Polytopes, and Pragmatic Distortion

Karkada et al. (2026) and Storan et al. (2026) prove that when co-occurrence statistics are invariant under a finite or compact group G (acting on a torsor or homogeneous space G/K), quadratic embeddings must lie in the irreducible representations (irreps) of G—explaining circular months (ℤ₁₂, per-month cosine 0.96), dihedral Bach triads (D₁₂, R² = 0.822), and spherical-harmonic celestial activations (SO(3), ℓ = 1, 2, 3).

Meanwhile, Park, Choe & Veitch (2024) and Park et al. (2024) show that hierarchical semantic concepts form linear directions and polytopes under a causal inner product, while a September 2026 formalization (The Linear Representation Hypothesis Needs a Group Action) demonstrates that LRH itself requires specifying a G-space and equivariant procedure. Unifying them with Huh et al. (2024)'s Platonic Representation Hypothesis yields one structural theorem: symmetric world relations induce irrep subspaces, taxonomic hierarchies induce polytope cones, and pragmatic compression (Zaslavsky et al. 2018) sets the perturbation floor between ideal geometry and empirical text.

Bridge B · Stage 2 → Stage 3

From Grounded Archives to Residual-Stream Belief Simplices

Why do deep Transformers acquire dynamic world models—spatiotemporal coordinates (Gurnee & Tegmark 2023), Othello board states (Li et al. 2023), and compositional skills (Arora & Goyal 2023)—rather than just static PMI tables? Because next-token prediction over an archive generated by grounded agents forces the network to track the latent state of the data-generating process.

Shai et al. (2024) proved that Transformers trained on Hidden Markov processes linearly represent the exact fractal geometry of the Bayesian belief simplex in their residual stream, while Xie et al. (2022) formalized in-context learning as implicit Bayesian inference over latent document concepts. Linking this to Grounded Misprision closes the loop: human archival re-reading (A6) and Transformer in-context inference are dual operations—both condition a belief distribution on retained forms, bounded above by the grounded information I(Ω; D_t) stored in the archive.

Bridge C · Stage 3 → Stage 4

CoT Deduction, Verifier Selection, and Rogers-Chain Dynamics

In the draft, deduction raises explicit content without raising grounded information (T4.3), and universality requires external memory (A9/T7). In LLMs, this corresponds exactly to inference-time Chain-of-Thought: Merrill & Sabharwal (2024) show that polynomial-length CoT elevates fixed-depth Transformers from constant-depth circuits to P-complete sequential computation, turning latent pretraining structure into explicit procedures.

Post-training then determines which branch of T3 dominates. Unfiltered self-training triggers Bayesian iterated-learning drift (Guo et al. 2026) and strong collapse unless data accumulate (Gerstgrasser et al. 2024; Dohmatob et al. 2024). Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards—exemplified by DeepSeek-R1 (2025)—supplies an external Δ_select gate that crosses Feng et al. (2025)'s verification threshold inside math and code. Outside verifiable domains, coupling human and machine loops forms a single Rogers social-learning chain (Nov 2026) whose equilibrium depends on the rate at which fresh human observation enters the corpus.

Statistical symmetry writes the world's geometry into the corpus; grounded selection keeps it alive across generations; next-token prediction recovers its belief manifold as a snapshot; and only externalized computation paired with independent verification can extend it.

This single causal law unifies all four stages: it turns the symbol-grounding debate, cultural-evolution ratchets, mechanistic interpretability, and LLM post-training into different regimes of the same information-theoretic and group-equivariant system.
